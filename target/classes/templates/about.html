About This Project

The Real-Time Smart Home Management System is a full-stack web application developed for users to organise their home into rooms, register smart devices under those rooms, and monitor the household's energy consumption all from a responsive web interface.

Devices can be switched ON or OFF from the dashboard. When a device is switched ON, its configured rated wattage contributes to the total load shown on the Live Power Consumption monitor. The energy figure (kWh) is estimated from the combined rated wattages of all currently active devices and the time they have been running — it is a software-based estimated value, not a reading from a physical sensor.
